## R2 — verified memory fix (run on this laptop, 8 Oct 2026)

```python
# vision/model.py (proposed) — load once; cast only real-valued weights of the two big encoders
model = build_sam3_image_model(checkpoint_path=CKPT, device="cpu", load_from_HF=False)
for sub in (model.backbone.vision_backbone.trunk, model.backbone.language_backbone.encoder):
    for p in sub.parameters():
        if p.is_floating_point():          # keep the 32 complex64 RoPE buffers intact
            p.data = p.data.to(torch.bfloat16)
model = model.cuda().eval()
# inference under torch.inference_mode() + torch.amp.autocast("cuda", dtype=torch.bfloat16)
```

| Variant (IMG_4151, 1024 px, 73 prompts) | Load VRAM | Peak allocated | Time | Detections | Mask IoU vs fp32 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Repo (fp32 weights + autocast) | 3.46 GB | 4.10 GB (spills) | 69.4 s incl. load | 23 | 1.00 (reference) |
| `model.to(bfloat16)` whole model | — | — | crashes in decoder (fp32 activations meet bf16 weights) | — | — |
| `.to(bfloat16)` on trunk + text encoder | 1.74 GiB | 2.31 GiB | 14.6 s | 2 | 0.04 (broken: `Module.to` strips the imaginary part of the RoPE buffers) |
| **Float params of trunk + text encoder → bf16** | **1.75 GiB** | **2.34 GiB (reserved 2.57)** | **15.7 s** | **22** | **0.955** |

Free VRAM at start was 3.23 GiB (Windows apps use about 0.8 GiB, not the 0.3 GiB the plan assumed). The fixed variant leaves roughly 0.9 GiB of headroom, enough to attempt the tiled pass the probe skipped.

- **N3** Zero-shot false positives remain (e.g. "crushing" on a signboard edge; soffit "spalling" covering 24–31% of the frame). This is expected before tuning and is why distress output stays unverified until G2.
- **N4** The tiled pass is worth it: on IMG_4231 the 1024-px pass found no distress, the native pass found 41 individual projecting rebars (T7.2-12) and 2 cracks at the girder end.
